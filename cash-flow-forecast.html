Cash Flow Forecast

A bank balance is not a forecast. This builds your 90-day cash flow map — what's coming in, what's going out, and when — so you see the gaps before they become crises. Estimates are fine; a rough forecast you actually look at beats a perfect one you never build.

Money coming in — next 90 days

Confirmed = signed contracts, retainers, work already booked. The money you'd bet on. Expected = your conservative pipeline floor — what you'd be surprised not to close, not what you're hoping for.
